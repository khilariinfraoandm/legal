Delete your KHILARI account

KHILARI is provided by Khilari Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. You can delete your account from the app, or ask us to do it by email.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open KHILARI and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out and your company administrators are notified.

Option 2: By email

If you can't open the app, email ssk.kipl2005@gmail.com from the email address registered on your account, with the subject "KHILARI account deletion". We may ask you to confirm your identity. We act on requests within 7 days.

What happens

  • Your account is deactivated immediately. During the next 7 days you can ask your company administrator or us to reactivate it.
  • After 7 days these are permanently deleted or anonymized: your name, email, phone number, profile photo, sign-in credentials and push notification token.
  • Forms, checklists and photos you submitted for your company are kept as business records, shown as "Deleted user", with no link to your identity. Your company can ask us to remove specific records.
  • If you are the only administrator of your company, you must first make someone else an administrator.
