Delete your account

KHILARI is provided by Khilari Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. Choose the option that suits you.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open KHILARI and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out and your company administrators are notified.

Option 2: By email

If you can't open the app, email us from the address registered on your account, with the subject "KHILARI account deletion". We may ask you to confirm your identity and act within 7 days.

Email ssk.kipl2005@gmail.com

What happens to your data

  • Your account is deactivated immediately. For 7 days you can ask your company administrator or us to reactivate it.
  • After 7 days these are permanently deleted or anonymized: your name, email, phone number, profile photo, sign-in credentials and push notification token.
  • Forms, checklists and photos you submitted for your company are kept as business records, shown as "Deleted user", with no link to your identity. Your company can ask us to remove specific records.
If you are the only administrator of your company, make someone else an administrator first. The last administrator can't delete their account.
