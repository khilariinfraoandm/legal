KHILARI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

KHILARI ("the app") is a workplace app for infrastructure and site-operations teams: employees submit checklists and forms, record attendance with a selfie, and attach site photos. It is provided by Khilari Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). Your employer (the "company") sets up your account and decides who in the company can see your data.

1. Data we collect

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data or use it for ads.

2. Face detection

When you take a selfie, the app checks that exactly one face is present using Google ML Kit running on your phone. No face template or biometric profile is created or stored. The resulting selfie image is uploaded like any other photo (see section 1).

3. Who can see your data

Your data is visible to you, to administrators of your company, and to our platform administrators who operate the service. It is not shared with other companies.

4. Service providers (processors)

  • Supabase – database, authentication, and private file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivers push notifications.
  • Google Sign-In – optional sign-in with Google.
  • Device geocoding service (provided by your phone's operating system) – converts coordinates to an address.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). These providers process data only on our behalf. Our Supabase project is hosted in Southeast Asia (Singapore), so your data is processed outside India.

5. Retention and deletion

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • Grace period: your account is deactivated immediately and your company administrators are notified. You can ask to be reactivated within 7 days.
  • After 7 days your personal data (name, email, phone, profile photo, sign-in credentials, notification token) is permanently removed or anonymized.
  • Submissions are kept, anonymized. Forms, checklists and photos you submitted for your company are retained as business records and shown as "Deleted user". Your company can ask us to remove specific records.
  • A company administrator's request follows the same 7-day period; the last administrator of a company cannot delete their account.
  • Without the app: see how to request deletion or email us.

Otherwise, data is kept while your company's account is active.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us (below) or your company administrator. If you are in a jurisdiction with data protection law (for example India's DPDP Act or the GDPR), you also have the rights it provides, including to complain to your regulator.

7. Children

The app is for working adults and is not directed to children under 18.

We will post changes here and update the date above.
