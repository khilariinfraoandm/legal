Who we are

KHILARI ("the app") is a workplace app for infrastructure and site-operations teams: employees submit checklists and forms, record attendance with a selfie, and attach site photos. It is provided by Khilari Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). Your employer (the "company") sets up your account and decides which of its administrators can see your data.

We use no advertising or tracking SDKs. We never sell your data or use it for ads.

Face detection

When you take a selfie, the app checks that exactly one face is present using Google ML Kit running on your phone. No face template or biometric profile is created or stored. The selfie image itself is uploaded like any other photo.

Who can see your data

You, the administrators of your company, and our platform administrators who operate the service. Other companies cannot see it.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, authentication and private file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications.
  • Google Sign-In: optional sign-in with Google.
  • Your phone's geocoding service: converts coordinates to an address.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). These providers process data only on our behalf. Our Supabase project is hosted in Southeast Asia (Singapore), so your data is processed outside India.

Retention and deletion

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account.
  • 7-day grace period: your account is deactivated immediately and your company administrators are notified. You can be reactivated within 7 days.
  • After 7 days your personal data (name, email, phone, profile photo, credentials, notification token) is permanently removed or anonymized.
  • Submissions are kept, anonymized. Forms, checklists and photos you submitted are retained as your company's business records and shown as "Deleted user". Your company can ask us to remove specific records.
  • A company administrator's request follows the same 7-day period; the last administrator of a company cannot delete their account.

Without the app: see how to request deletion, or email us. Otherwise, data is kept while your company's account is active.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us or your company administrator. You also have the rights given by the data protection law that applies to you (for example India's DPDP Act or the GDPR), including the right to complain to your regulator.

Children: KHILARI is for working adults and is not directed to children under 18.
